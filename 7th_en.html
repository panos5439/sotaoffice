🔥 UNIT 7: FIRE SAFETY & EMERGENCY

Fire & evacuation: Prevention and Calmness

🎯 Goal: to know how to prevent fires, how to react safely and what to do in case of an alarm or evacuation.

Prevention saves – calmness protects.

7.1 Fire prevention in the office

Prevention is always the best “firefighting”.

✅ To avoid a fire:

  • Do not overload sockets and power strips.
  • Do not leave devices (coffee machines, heaters, printers) on when you leave.
  • Do not cover electrical devices or ventilation openings.
  • Do not store paper near heaters or sockets.
  • Do not smoke outside designated areas.
  • Keep corridors and emergency exits clear.

7.2 Knowing the fire safety equipment

Every employee should know:

  • Where the fire extinguishers and fire points are located.
  • Where the emergency exits and escape routes are.
  • Where the assembly point outside the building is.

7.3 Types of fire extinguishers

Type Suitable for Attention
CO₂ (Carbon dioxide) Fires from electrical equipment Leaves no residue – be careful with hands (very low temperature).
Dry powder Multi-purpose (paper, textiles, liquids, electrical). Strong extinguishing power, but leaves powder residue.
Foam Liquid fuels (e.g. petrol, oil). Not to be used on electrical fires.

7.4 How to use an extinguisher (P.A.S.S. rule)

  • P – Pull: Pull the safety pin.
  • A – Aim: Aim at the base of the fire.
  • S – Squeeze: Squeeze the handle.
  • S – Sweep: Sweep horizontally with small movements.

Always keep a distance of 2–3 metres and keep your back towards the exit.

7.5 Actions in case of fire

  • Stay calm — panic causes accidents.
  • Raise the alarm immediately (verbally or via alarm).
  • Call 112 or inform the responsible staff.
  • If safe, use a fire extinguisher.
  • Do not try to put out a large fire alone.
  • Evacuate via the nearest emergency exit.
  • Do not use elevators.
  • Close doors behind you to contain the fire.

7.6 Evacuation (Escape plan)

  • Do not run; move quickly but calmly.
  • Leave all personal belongings behind.
  • If you wear high heels, take them off.
  • Do not stop for questions or photos.
  • Guide visitors or colleagues towards the exit.
  • Once outside, go to the designated assembly point.
  • Do not re-enter the building until officially allowed.

7.7 After evacuation

  • Go to the team’s assembly point.
  • Answer to your name when roll call is made.
  • Report if someone is missing or needs help.
  • Do not leave the assembly area without instruction.

Example

At company “Alpha”, an evacuation drill was carried out. Employees who knew the emergency exits needed only 2 minutes to evacuate. Those who did not know where to go delayed and created congestion. This is why drills and knowledge of the evacuation plan are vital.

🟢 “Prevention saves – calmness protects. In fire or emergency, life comes first.”
