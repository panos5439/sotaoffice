🌍 EARTHQUAKE GUIDE: PROTECTION & EVACUATION

What to do during an earthquake at the office

🎯 Goal: To know exactly what to do before, during and after an earthquake, and how to evacuate office spaces safely and in an organized way.

Rule: Drop – Cover – Hold on.

Important: Aftershocks are common. Evacuate only after shaking stops and it is safe to move.

1) Before an earthquake (office preparedness)

What I should know

  • Where emergency exits and stairs are.
  • Where the assembly point is.
  • Where the first aid kit is.

What I keep safe

  • Avoid heavy objects on high shelves.
  • Secure bookcases/monitors where possible.
  • Keep corridors and exits clear.

Roles (if assigned)

  • Evacuation warden: guides the flow toward the Emergency exit.
  • First aiders: assist with minor injuries.
  • Roll-call point: confirms people are accounted for.

2) During the earthquake

If I’m at a desk

  • Drop and get under a sturdy desk.
  • Protect head/neck with arms.
  • Hold on to the desk leg until shaking stops.

If I’m in a corridor/open area

  • Move to an interior wall, away from glass.
  • Drop and protect head/neck.
  • I do not run toward stairs while the vibrations last.

What I avoid

  • Do not use elevators.
  • Avoid shelves/cabinets/lights.
  • Don’t rush outside during shaking.

If you’re near a window: move away immediately and protect your head.

3) Immediately after (check & prepare to evacuate)

Quick check

  • Check yourself and others for injuries.
  • Look for hazards: broken glass, fallen items.
  • Listen for instructions/announcements if available.

If there are danger signs

  • Gas smell/smoke → move away and report.
  • Down wires/sparks → don’t touch, keep distance.
  • Cracks/falling debris → avoid passing underneath.

Aftershocks

  • Can happen at any time.
  • If an aftershock starts, stop and cover again.
  • Keep exits in mind, stay calm.

4) Office evacuation (step-by-step)

Step 1: Start calmly

  • Take only essentials (e.g., phone), no delays.
  • Close doors behind you, do not lock.
  • Move in order, no pushing.

Step 2: Stairs & route

  • Use stairs only (no elevators).
  • Hold the handrail, watch your steps.
  • Follow escape routes/emergency exits.

Step 3: Assembly point

  • Stay away from buildings, facades, glass.
  • Stay at the assembly point until instructions are given.
  • Roll-call is done; report missing persons.

What I don’t do

  • Do not re-enter without official clearance.
  • Don’t block stairs/exits to “wait”.
  • Don’t stand under balconies/signs.

⚠️ If there is smoke/fire, follow fire safety procedures and change route if needed.

5) Special situations

If someone has mobility difficulties

  • Ask for help from the assigned support/warden.
  • Move calmly to a safer area/stairwell.
  • Don’t attempt risky carries without guidance.

If I’m trapped

  • Stay calm and call for help if possible.
  • Cover mouth/nose from dust, avoid unnecessary movement.
  • Make periodic noise (not constantly) to help rescuers locate you.

Communication

  • Prefer short texts instead of calls (reduces network load).
  • Follow company/warden instructions.
  • Avoid rumors—verify information.

Quick reminder (checklist)

During shaking

  • Drop
  • Cover
  • Hold on

After shaking

  • Check injuries
  • Watch hazards
  • Evacuate via stairs

At assembly point

  • Roll-call
  • Report issues
  • Wait for instructions
