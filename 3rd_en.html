UNIT 3: GENERAL SAFETY RULES

General Safety Rules

🎯 Goal: To know and apply the basic principles of safe behaviour from the moment we arrive at the office until the moment we leave.

Safety is not an accident. It is a choice and a daily practice.

Mindset · Rules · Behaviour

3.1 Safe mindset

Safety is a way of thinking. It is not something we remember only after an accident, but a habit we apply every day.

  • I keep my eyes open for hazards (e.g. cables, water, obstacles).
  • I think before acting – “is what I’m about to do safe?”
  • If I can, I fix something dangerous. If not, I inform the responsible person.
  • I check whether the problem has been solved – I don’t leave it “for later”.

3.2 Following rules & instructions

  • I always apply the company’s safety rules.
  • I read and follow the instructions given for using equipment or procedures.
  • If I am unsure about something, I ask my supervisor or the Safety Officer – I do not assume.
  • I work with focus, without rushing or distractions (e.g. phone, food, chatting while using equipment).

3.3 Reporting unsafe conditions

No hazard is “too small”. If I notice something that could cause an accident, I must report it.

Frayed cable I inform IT or maintenance.
Obstacle in an emergency exit I immediately report it so it can be removed.
Loose socket or unattended heater I point it out to the supervisor or technical team.

Better a “false alarm” than an injury.

3.4 Behaviour at the office

  • I do not push, run or play in the workplace.
  • I do not climb on chairs or desks to reach something – I use a step ladder.
  • I do not block walkways or emergency exits with boxes or chairs.
  • I keep drawers closed – this prevents tripping.
  • I use the handrail on stairs.
  • If I see a leak or water, I clean it up or report it immediately.

3.5 Personal behaviour

  • We respect colleagues and their instructions.
  • We do not ignore mistakes or dangerous actions by others – we inform them politely.
  • We actively participate in evacuation drills and trainings.

💬 Example — Maria in the hallway

Maria noticed that a printer cable was running across the hallway. Instead of ignoring it, she informed the technical department and the problem was solved. A potential slip or fall was avoided.
