Portable Fire Extinguisher Maintenance
🧯 MAINTENANCE & USE OF PORTABLE EXTINGUISHERS

An extinguisher is useful only if it is ready to work

Proper maintenance ensures that an extinguisher will work reliably when we need it. In this unit we see how often it must be checked, when the agent is replaced and what its service life is.

An extinguisher is not decoration – it is life-saving equipment.

1. Annual maintenance by a certified company

Proper maintenance is essential for an extinguisher to work reliably and effectively in an emergency. It must be serviced once a year by a recognised and certified maintenance company.

The pressure gauge shows if the extinguisher is properly pressurized (green zone).

2. Quarterly visual inspection by the owner

In addition to annual servicing, the owner must carry out a quarterly visual inspection, ensuring that:

  • The pressure gauge needle is in the green zone.
  • There are no visible damages, rust or deformation on the body.
  • The safety pin is in place and intact.

If any issue is found, you must immediately contact the maintenance company for repair or replacement.

3. Extinguishing agent & extended inspection

The extinguishing agent must be replaced every 5 years, regardless of the condition of the extinguisher, or immediately after any use.

If the extinguisher is older than 10 years, or when its general condition requires it, it is subject to an extended laboratory inspection, including:

  • Replacement of all worn parts.
  • Replacement of the extinguishing agent.
  • Hydraulic pressure test of the cylinder.
  • Full inspection of the pressure system.

4. Extinguisher service life

Powder extinguishers have a maximum service life of 20 years. After this period they must be withdrawn and replaced with new ones.

5. Fire rating – how “strong” is the extinguisher?

The performance of an extinguisher is called its fire rating and is printed on its label. Two extinguishers with the same amount of agent do not necessarily have the same effectiveness – therefore the rating must be carefully considered when selecting the right extinguisher for each area.
