UNIT 4: HOUSEKEEPING – SLIPS – TRIPS – FALLS

Housekeeping & Safe Walking in the Office

🎯 Goal: To recognise hazards caused by clutter, poor organisation and rush, and to apply simple prevention rules for a safe office.

A clean and tidy workspace = a safe workspace.

Example of a slip caused by a cable/clutter — one small mistake is enough.

4.1 What does “housekeeping” mean

Housekeeping means “a tidy workspace”. A tidy space is safer, more functional and more pleasant.

Why is it important?

  • It reduces slips and falls.
  • It facilitates evacuation in case of danger.
  • It creates a professional and clean environment.

4.2 Common causes of accidents in offices

  • Objects or cables on the floor.
  • Open drawers or doors.
  • Liquids on the floor (e.g. coffee, water, cleaning products).
  • Insufficient lighting.
  • Obstacles in escape routes or on stairs.
  • Broken chairs or loose/slippery rugs.

These “small” mistakes are the number one causes of falls in offices.

4.3 Practical prevention rules

✅ Daily:

  • We keep desks and floors clear of papers, boxes, cables or bags.
  • We dispose of waste only in appropriate bins.
  • We clean up spills immediately (or inform the cleaning staff).
  • We check the floor regularly for moisture or damage.
  • We do not place objects on stairs or in walkways.
  • Do not let power cables cross walkways.
  • Do not overfill storage areas or shelves – items may fall.
  • Keep drawers and cabinets closed.

4.4 Tips for safe movement

  • When walking in corridors, avoid carrying so many items that they block your view.
  • If you need something from a high shelf → do not climb on a chair or desk, use a step ladder with non-slip feet.
  • Do not leave bags or cables under your desk, as your feet may get caught.
  • Areas must be adequately lit – poor lighting leads to tripping.
  • Always use handrails when going up or down stairs.

4.5 Practical example

Nikos was running to catch a phone call and slipped on a spot where water had leaked from the air-condition unit. If that spot had been cleaned or a “CAUTION – SLIPPERY FLOOR” sign had been placed, the accident would have been avoided.

4.6 What to do if I notice a problem

  • If I see something dangerous (e.g. broken tile, loose rug, leak), I do not ignore it.
  • I immediately inform maintenance or my supervisor.
  • If there is an imminent danger, I warn my colleagues.

🟢 “A clean and tidy space = a safe space.” One minute of tidying can prevent an injury.
