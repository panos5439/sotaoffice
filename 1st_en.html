Introduction to OHS

🎯 Goal: Understand why OHS matters to everyone, including office workers.

What is OHS?

Occupational Health & Safety includes rules, actions, and behaviors that aim to prevent accidents, protect health, and improve workers’ wellbeing.

Why does it matter?

Many people think offices are 'safe spaces', but there are hazards such as:

  • Falls from chairs or ladders
  • Musculoskeletal problems from poor posture
  • Eye strain and headaches
  • Electrical hazards (cables, sockets, equipment)
  • Stress and workload pressure

Accidents don’t only happen on construction sites — they also happen in offices.

What do we gain from prevention?

Better physical and mental health
Fewer absences from work
Higher productivity
Pleasant and safe environment
Positive work climate

Everyday examples

Chair without lumbar support

Leads to lower back pain after weeks.

Cable on the floor

Increases the chance of tripping.

Poor lighting

Causes eye fatigue and more mistakes.

🟢 Safety starts with our attitude. Whatever we do, we do it carefully and responsibly.
