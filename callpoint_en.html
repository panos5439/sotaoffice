🚨 MANUAL CALL POINTS (FIRE ALARM BUTTONS)

They trigger the alarm immediately in an emergency

🎯 Goal: To learn what a manual call point is, where it is located, when to use it, and what steps to follow after activation.

Rule: Call point → Inform → Evacuate.

Example manual call point

Fire alarm manual call point (manual activation).

It is usually red and placed in visible locations near emergency exits and escape routes.

What it is, where it is, when & how to use it

1) What it is

  • A device for manually activating the fire alarm.
  • It sends a signal to the fire alarm control panel.
  • It activates sirens/beacons depending on the system.

2) Where it is usually located

  • Near emergency exits and stairwells.
  • In corridors and common areas.
  • Not blocked by objects/furniture.

3) When to use it

  • When I see fire or smoke.
  • When there is immediate danger and the alarm hasn’t activated.
  • During evacuation, if I pass a call point and the alarm hasn’t sounded.

4) How to use it

  • Activate it (press or break the protective part, depending on the type).
  • Warn people nearby and call 112 (or delegate to someone).
  • Follow the green exit signs and evacuate to the assembly point.

⚠️ Use only in real emergencies. False activation may cause panic and disrupt safety response.
