UNIT 2: EMPLOYER & EMPLOYEE DUTIES

Employer & Employee Duties

🎯 Goal: To clearly understand the duties and responsibilities of both employer and employee regarding safety and health in the workplace.

The employer’s role

The employer has the overall responsibility to ensure that everyone works safely and without risks to their health.

The employee’s role

Every employee: protecting themselves and their colleagues.

2.1 Employer Duties

The employer has the overall responsibility to ensure that all employees work safely and without risks to their health. Specifically, the employer must:

  • Evaluates the risks present in the workplace (e.g. electricity, ergonomics, lighting).
  • Take preventive measures to avoid accidents or health problems.
  • Provide training and information to all employees about risks, proper equipment use, and behaviour in emergencies.
  • Provide suitable Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) when needed (e.g. gloves, masks, goggles, earplugs, high-visibility vest).
  • Ensure proper maintenance of equipment and facilities.
  • Provide a Safety Engineer and Occupational Physician when required.
  • Report accidents and dangerous incidents to the relevant authorities.
  • Keep written safety measures and plans (e.g. evacuation plan, accident log).

2.2 Employee Duties

No employee is a passive recipient of rules. Each worker takes care of his own safety, but also of the safety of his colleagues. They must:

  • Follow OHS rules and the instructions of the employer or Safety Engineer.
  • Use devices, tools and equipment correctly.
  • Do not bypass or deactivate safety mechanisms (e.g. covers, safety switches).
  • Immediately report any problem or hazardous situation (e.g. frayed cable, leak, blocked emergency exit).
  • Keep their workspace clean and tidy.
  • Use and properly maintain the PPE provided.
  • Attend safety trainings.
  • Cooperate with colleagues and supervisors to maintain a safe environment.

2.3 Practical examples

Different situations at work require action from both the employer and the employee.

Situation The employer must… The employee must…
Computers and chairs Provide ergonomic equipment Adjust the chair correctly
Electrical equipment Maintain and inspect sockets and cables Do not overload multi-sockets
Fire Provide fire extinguishers and training Know their location and the emergency exits
Work area Organise cleaning and inspections Keep the area tidy

🔸 Safety is not only a management issue. It is everyone’s responsibility – employer and employees – to create and maintain a safe environment.
