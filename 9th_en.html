🚧 UNIT 9: SAFETY SIGNAGE

Signage is the “voice” of safety

🎯 Goal: To learn what safety signs in the workplace mean, how to react when we see them and why we must never ignore or move them.

If you understand signage, you see danger before it reaches you.

9.1 What is safety signage

Safety signage is a system of symbols, colours and shapes that helps us quickly understand what danger exists and what we must do.

It is internationally recognised so that everyone —regardless of language— can understand the message.

9.2 General rules

  • I know the signs that exist in my workplace.
  • I strictly follow what they instruct.
  • I report if any sign is missing, damaged or not visible.
  • I never cover or move signs (e.g. behind furniture or cabinets).
  • I do not remove labels from chemicals or equipment.

9.3 Categories of safety signs

Each category has a specific colour and shape so we can recognise it instantly:

Warning signs
Yellow triangle · warns of danger (e.g. risk of electric shock).
Prohibition signs
Red circle with diagonal line · something is forbidden (e.g. smoking).
Mandatory signs
Blue circle · you must do something (e.g. wear gloves).
Escape / safety signs
Green rectangle · shows emergency exit or first aid.
Fire-fighting equipment signs
Red square · indicates extinguisher, fire hose, etc.

9.4 Special labels and markings

Chemical substances

Each container has a GHS label with hazard symbols (e.g. flammable, corrosive, toxic). We never remove these labels.

Exits and escape routes

Green signs above doors indicate escape routes. These routes must always be clear of obstacles.

Electrical panels & storage areas

Electrical panels have warning signs for voltage. Storage areas often have signs for restricted access or obligatory PPE.

9.5 Practical tips

  • Look around you: do you know where the nearest emergency exit is?
  • If you see “CAUTION – SLIPPERY FLOOR”, do not ignore it.
  • If a sign is missing from an extinguisher or door, report it immediately.
  • In an evacuation drill, follow the green exit signs.

🟢 “Signage is the ‘voice’ of safety. If you know it and listen to it, you prevent danger.”
